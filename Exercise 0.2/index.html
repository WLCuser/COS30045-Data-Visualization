FAQ section

Question 1: What is the top three highest energy guzzling appliances in aussie homes?

Air Conditioning, Electric Water Heater and Refrigerator

Question 2: Why air conditoning consumes so much?

Cooling large spaces and constantly maintaining a set temperature demands a lot of energy, particularly when outdoor temperatures are high.

Question 3: Why electric water heater consumes so much?

Heating water requires a lot of energy, and traditional tank-style heaters must keep a large volume of water hot at all times.

Question 4: Why refrigerator consumes so much?

Constantly maintaining a cold temperature requires significant energy, especially in warm kitchens or when the door is frequently opened.

Question 5: What is the other factor that increase the energy consumption on appliances?

It is standby power (also known as vampire power) is power drawn by an appliance when it is not in use. Standby power can account for up to 3% of your household energy use and could cost you up to $100 a year.
